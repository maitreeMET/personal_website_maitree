I am a student in UC Berkeley's **Management, Entrepreneurship & Technology (M.E.T.)** dual-degree program, studying **Electrical Engineering & Computer Science** and **Business Administration** at the Haas School of Business.

I do machine learning research at **Berkeley AI Research (BAIR)** in the EMBER Centre, advised by Jonas Frey and Siming He in the labs of Prof. Jitendra Malik and Prof. Claire Tomlin. My research focuses on **vision-language navigation** — training agents to find objects under a time budget. This work won the **Best Paper Award** at the IROS 2026 Workshop on Intelligent Information Gathering, and an extended version is under review at ICRA 2027.

Before BAIR, I was a machine learning research intern at **Relling Systems**, where I studied temporal blindness in robot foundation models (π0-FAST, π0.5). I have also worked as a quantitative research intern at **Squarepoint Capital** and **Engelhart Trading**, and as a software engineer at **LinkedIn** and **Handle.com**.

Outside of work I enjoy running, table tennis, hiking, and mathematics.
