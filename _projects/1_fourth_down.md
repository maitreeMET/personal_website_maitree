**Fourth Down** is a live event-trading dashboard for college football prediction markets. It streams market odds and play-by-play data into a single interface with sub-second updates.

- Reacted to large in-game price swings within **300–400 ms**
- Grew a dedicated **$15k** bankroll to **$62k** over 8 weeks (**313% ROI**)
